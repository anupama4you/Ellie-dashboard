FAQ

## Common questions.

Will customers know Ellie is AI?


Most callers don't ask — Ellie sounds warm and natural. If someone does ask directly, she's honest about it and keeps helping with their enquiry.

Can I keep my existing phone number?


In most setups Ellie answers on a number that forwards from your existing line, so you don't need to reprint signage or update Google. We'll confirm the exact setup for your business during onboarding.

What happens if Ellie doesn't know an answer?


She doesn't guess. Ellie takes a message and flags it for your team rather than inventing pricing, hours or details she hasn't been given.

Can Ellie transfer calls to me?


Intelligent live call transfer is coming soon. Right now, if a call needs a human, Ellie takes a detailed message and notifies your team so you can follow up quickly.

Can Ellie book appointments?


Yes. Ellie checks your calendar availability, books appointments directly into supported calendars, and sends an SMS confirmation to the customer.

Does Ellie work after hours?


Yes — Ellie answers 24/7, including nights, weekends and public holidays, so after-hours enquiries never go to voicemail.

Can I choose when Ellie answers?


Yes. Ellie can be your full-time receptionist, or step in only after hours or when a call is missed — you decide.

Can Ellie handle multiple calls at once?


Yes — because Ellie runs in the cloud rather than as a single physical line, she can answer more than one call at a time, so customers don't hit a busy signal.

Can I listen to calls?


Yes. Every call is recorded and available in your dashboard, so you can review exactly what was said.

Can I cancel anytime?


Yes. There's no lock-in contract — it's month-to-month, and you can cancel anytime.

How does the 7-day trial work?

What happens after the founding offer?

Is my customer data secure?


Calls and business details are handled with privacy in mind. Read our [Privacy Policy](https://callellie.com/privacy) for exactly what's collected and how it's used.

How quickly can I get set up?
